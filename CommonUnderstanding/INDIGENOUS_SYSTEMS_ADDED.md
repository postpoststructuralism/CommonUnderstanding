## Common Themes Across Indigenous Systems

### Universal Patterns

1. **Sacred Relationship with Land**
   - Land is not owned but cared for
   - Specific territories hold spiritual significance
   - Ancestors connected to place

2. **Animism/Spirit World**
   - All of nature has spirit/consciousness
   - Animals as teachers and kin
   - Plants, stones, elements as alive

3. **Reciprocity and Balance**
   - Give and take with nature/spirits
   - Maintain cosmic harmony
   - Respect leads to abundance

4. **Shamanic Intermediaries**
   - Specialists journey to spirit worlds
   - Healing through spiritual means
   - Drums, songs, plants as tools

5. **Oral Tradition**
   - Knowledge passed through stories
   - Elders as wisdom keepers
   - Sacred songs and chants

6. **Cyclical Time**
   - Ceremonies mark seasons
   - Renewal and regeneration
   - Past, present, future interconnected

7. **Interconnection**
   - All beings related
   - Web of relationships
   - Human responsibility to maintain balance

8. **Sacred Sites and Objects**
   - Mountains, rivers, specific places holy
   - Ceremonial tools (drums, pipes, etc.)
   - Art as spiritual expression

### Match Calculation

Indigenous systems are matched using:
- **Value keywords** extracted from CorePrinciples
- **Inferred Moral Foundations** from description/principles
- **Match percentage** (0-100%) across three dimensions

## Moral Foundation Inference

Since most indigenous systems don't have explicit MF scores, they're inferred:

### Care/Harm
Keywords: compassion, kindness, love, mercy, empathy
- **High in**: San (healing focus), Lakota (Mitakuye Oyasin), Hawaiian (aloha)

### Fairness/Cheating
Keywords: justice, equality, rights, reciprocity
- **High in**: Haudenosaunee (democratic law), Andean (ayni reciprocity)

### Loyalty/Betrayal
Keywords: community, solidarity, collective, tradition
- **High in**: Hawaiian (ohana/family), Yoruba (clan structure), M?ori (whanaungatanga)

### Authority/Subversion
Keywords: hierarchy, order, elders, respect
- **High in**: Yoruba (Orishas hierarchy), Ainu (elder respect), Cherokee (medicine authority)

### Sanctity/Degradation
Keywords: sacred, holy, pure, divine, spiritual
- **High in**: All indigenous systems (high reverence for sacred)

### Liberty/Oppression
Keywords: freedom, autonomy, choice, self-determination
- **Variable**: Haudenosaunee (high), Hawaiian (moderate with kapu system)
