# Master Math Plan v2 — Multiplication/Division Focus, Real Lesson Schedule

This supersedes `master_plan_consolidated`. Two changes from the prior version: (1) addition/subtraction is now a **5-minute warm-up game**, not the main lesson focus — multiplication review and division fluency are the core work; (2) all scheduling is rebuilt around actual lesson days: **Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday only**.

---

## PART 1: Standing Lesson Structure (every lesson day, going forward)

**Warm-up (5 min) — addition/subtraction, kept light, game-based:**
Rotate through: card War, bonds-to-10/20 dice game, or a quick verbal "partner to 10" round. This exists purely to keep the foundation warm, not to remediate it — no worksheets, no correction pileup, just quick reps.

**Main focus (15–20 min) — multiplication review → division fluency:**
Since her times tables were solid as of last semester, start each block with a fast multiplication spot-check (not a drill — just confirm the facts are still there), then move into the actual division work: Tier A/B/C from the practice worksheet, number-bond regrouping, and eventually the area-model bridge.

**Closer (5–10 min, 1–2x per week, not every day):**
Coin/blackjack session for money-value fluency. Doesn't need to happen every lesson day — twice a week is plenty.

## PART 2: THIS FIRST WEEK OF LESSONS (5 lesson days: Fri Sept 25, Mon Sept 28, Tue Sept 29, Thu Oct 1, Fri Oct 2)

### Today — Friday, September 25
- **Reset (once, briefly, before the lesson block):** confusion isn't disappointment — say it plainly, not as a big sit-down.
- **Warm-up (5 min):** War with a standard deck.
- **Main focus (15 min):** Quick multiplication spot-check across a few random facts (e.g., 6×7, 8×4, 9×6) — this doubles as a diagnostic for whether the regression touched multiplication at all, which matters given last semester's fluency. Then straight into **Tier A division** (2-digit ÷ 1-digit), framed as "let's see what you've still got."
- **No abacus, no closer today.** Keep today short and low-pressure since it's also the reset day.

### Monday, September 28
- **Warm-up (5 min):** Abacus 5/6 recalibration — show 5 (blue), add 1 yellow for 6, narrate "5 and 1," jump around rather than counting in strict order.
- **Main focus (20 min):** Multiplication spot-check (fast), then **Tier A division**, aiming for full completion of Tier A without hesitation.
- **Closer (10 min):** First coin/blackjack session — nickels, dimes, quarters only; quick coin-exchange warm-up first ("how many nickels make a quarter?").

### Tuesday, September 29
- **Warm-up (5 min):** Bonds-to-10/20 dice game.
- **Main focus (20 min):** Move into **Tier B division** if Tier A felt solid Monday; if not, more Tier A with the area-model rectangle sketched alongside for the first time as a visual bridge.
- **No closer today** — keep Tuesday slightly lighter.

### Thursday, October 1
- **Warm-up (5 min):** Card game or verbal bonds round, whichever she's enjoyed most so far.
- **Main focus (20 min):** **Tier B/C division**, explicitly using the area-model rectangle framing on at least one problem (e.g., 276 ÷ 4 → split into 240+36).
- **Closer (10 min):** Second coin/blackjack session, now adding pennies.

### Friday, October 2
- **Warm-up (5 min):** Quick multiplication spot-check as a warm-up this time (rotate its position so it doesn't always signal "the hard part is starting").
- **Main focus (20 min):** Mini checkpoint — mixed Tier A–C division problems — to decide whether Tier C becomes the default starting point next week.
- **Closer (5 min):** Name one specific thing she did well this week — concrete, not generic ("the way you split 483 into 420+63 was exactly right").

**Success markers for the month:** multiplication facts stay fast and confident throughout (confirms the earlier regression was addition/subtraction-specific, not a broader fact-retrieval issue); division Tier progression moves at roughly one tier per lesson block without the earlier hesitation/shutdown pattern; coin games feel like play, not a lesson.

## What Changed From v1 (for your records)

- Addition/subtraction downgraded from "primary focus of Week 1" to a standing 5-minute warm-up across all future lessons — still present, no longer the main event.
- Multiplication added explicitly as a quick spot-check at the start of each main block, since it was previously assumed fine but never actually re-verified after the regression was discovered.
- All scheduling rebuilt around Mon/Tue/Thu/Fri lesson days; no more calendar-week structure that assumed daily lessons.
- Division is now the clear centre of gravity for this first lesson block, with Tiers A→C as the visible progress marker.

`master_plan_consolidated` is now superseded by this document for Parts 4–6 (month/today/next week). Its Parts 1–3 (principles, verified curriculum codes, full-year map) are unaffected by this change and remain the standing reference.
